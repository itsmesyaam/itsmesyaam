## About Me

<img align="right" width="300" src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Inter&weight=700&size=18&duration=2200&pause=600&color=5E9BFF&vCenter=true&multiline=true&width=300&height=90&lines=Business+%E2%86%92+Specs;Constraints+%E2%86%92+Decisions;Data+%E2%86%92+Direction" alt="Business to specs, constraints to decisions, data to direction"/>

I'm a **Technical Project Manager & Business Analyst** (B.Tech CS) based in **Kochi, India**, currently at **Bell & Ring Tech Nirman Pvt. Ltd.**

I sit at the intersection of engineering and business. I don't just track sprints. I translate ambiguous business needs into technical specs, and I translate technical constraints back into business decisions stakeholders can act on.

I work across **React / TypeScript / Python** product teams, and I drive decisions using **Power BI, Tableau and SQL** rather than gut feel.
